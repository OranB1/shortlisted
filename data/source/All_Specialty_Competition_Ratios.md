# UK Specialty Training — All-Specialty Competition Ratios & Selection Mechanisms (2024–2025, official NHS England)

*Companion file to the v2 compendium. Pulled directly from NHS England's official "Competition ratios" archive (medical.hee.nhs.uk), which publishes clean year-by-year tables for every specialty in one place — a better primary source than the specialty-by-specialty pages used earlier. Use this as the master ratio table; the v2 compendium's Part 1–3 IMT deep-dive still stands as the more detailed source for IMT specifically.*

## How each specialty selects (confirmed mechanisms)

| Specialty | Selection mechanism |
|---|---|
| IMT / joint IMT-ACCS-IM | Self-assessment only, max 30 (+5 IMT-only bonus in 2026 = max 35). No MSRA. Final rank by interview. |
| GP ST1 | 100% MSRA (no Selection Centre, no interview, 2025/2026) |
| Core Psychiatry CT1 | 100% MSRA (no interview, 2025/2026) |
| ACCS-Emergency Medicine ST1 | MSRA 40% / online interview 60% |
| Anaesthetics CT1 (incl. ACCS-Anaesthetics) | MSRA 15% (PD 7.5% + CPS 7.5%) / interview 85% |
| Core Surgical Training CT1 | MSRA 10% / portfolio station 45% / management & clinical interview station 45% (portfolio scored at interview, not pre-scored) |
| Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Clinical Radiology, Ophthalmology, Neurosurgery, Community Sexual & Reproductive Health, Histopathology, Public Health | MSRA used for shortlisting/bypass-to-offer; exact weighting varies — check each specialty's own page for current-year weighting |
| MSRA minimum standard | 201 on each of the 2 components (PD and CPS) — falling below on either is an automatic fail regardless of specialty |

**Pattern**: every single Round 1 specialty got harder 2024→2025, several dramatically (ACCS-EM nearly doubled, Anaesthetics nearly doubled, O&G more than doubled, Core Psychiatry more than doubled). GP and IMT — the two priority routes for the app — rose more moderately (+34% and +43% respectively) than the surgical/EM/psychiatry routes.

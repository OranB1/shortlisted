# Call transcript: Jemma Kelly, Chelsea & Westminster QI team

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Meeting title:** Medical student QI platform — pilot integration with Jemma
**Participants:** Oran Barak [um23ob], Jemma Kelly (Transformation Specialist Lead / Clinical Quality Improvement Advisor, Chelsea & Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust)

**Context:** Nolan Arulraj (C&W) replied to an initial outreach email saying "it sounds like a good idea" and pointed to two inboxes for approval — audits (`chelwest.clinicalgovernance@nhs.net`) and QIPs (`chelwest.improvement@nhs.net`). That thread was forwarded to the QI team, which is how this call came about. This is a first fact-finding call, not a pitch — used to validate the "opportunity marketplace" concept directly against how QI/audit actually moves through a real NHS trust, and to figure out how a pilot could plug into existing infrastructure (the trust's RIQI — Research, Innovation and Quality Improvement — portal) rather than compete with it.

This transcript is preserved verbatim (lightly cleaned of filler/false starts is *not* applied — this is the raw transcript) as the primary source behind the decision to pivot the product from portfolio-scoring-first to opportunity-matching-marketplace-first (see `CLAUDE.md` for the resulting product decisions). Key things this call surfaced, expanded on in `CLAUDE.md`:

- Confirmed the core problem is completion, not ideation: students/trainees pick either over-ambitious or too-small projects, and once they rotate off, the project dies — "you get told you have to do it... but you've only got three months."
- C&W has a repository/registration system for QI projects but adoption is poor — "people are not registering projects... the hospital have no idea on all these projects that are being done."
- No follow-up/completion tracking currently exists at the trust level — "Can I tell you how many of those are successful? No."
- Real interest in cross-trust visibility/comparison — C&W is actively trying to collaborate with Hillingdon, Imperial, London Northwest and sees value in seeing what other trusts' QI projects look like, explicitly to avoid reinventing the wheel: *"if Imperial did a really good project, we want to learn from it and just copy them."*
- Open questions raised by Jemma that need resolving before a pilot: governance (especially given AI in the shortlisting step), and whether an external (non-Trust-employee) team can operate a tool used by Trust staff. She's taking these to "Mike," the head of innovation, before anything further.
